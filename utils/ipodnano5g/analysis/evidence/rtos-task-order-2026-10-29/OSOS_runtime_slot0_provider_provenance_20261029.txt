2. FUN_08000a10 calls FUN_0800196c with the ATA/block-provider object and

3. FUN_0800196c classifies a sector whose MBR signature word at +0x1fe is

   0x06, 0x0b, 0x0c, and 0x0e, checks nonzero start/count and bounds count by

4. On successful detection, FUN_08000a10 calls FUN_0818e998 with slot ID 0,

   FUN_08110e30, the FAT-provider constructor, and FUN_0818e998 stores the
   resulting service pointer at registry[0].
5. The `FUN_08110e30` VTable has the file-open method at slot +0x14 pointing

Conclusion and limits
  This statically resolves the previously unknown provider class: if the
  DeviceDiscovery queue event reaches case 0 and LBA 0 passes MBR/partition
  validation, slot 0 is populated with a FAT provider. A prior archived-volume
  analysis records an 0xaa55 MBR and the type-2 path. This does not observe the
  queue delivery, successful registration on the device, live filesystem
  state, Open/Read of `accessories.reg` or `/BOOT.BIN`, parser execution, or
  Rockbox handoff. The status/return-value behavior of all lower ATA services
  is not dynamically tested here.
