## Stack ledger

All SP values are relative to S, the parser frame base. The `0x44` field
starts at S+4, so a word at S+n occupies field data offset n-4.

After the constructor/trampoline segment already accounted for in
OSOS_accessories_stack_subgadgets_20261029.txt, the second stack-relative
entry has SP=S+0x1b0 and PC=0x0800396d.

1. `0x0800396d` sets r0=S+0x1b0. The `bx lr` helper preserves r0; its pop
   consumes six words and leaves SP=S+0x1c8.
2. Two `0x08262d53` entries subtract 0x8c each and pop four words each.
   They leave r0=S+0x98 and SP=S+0x1e8.
3. `0x081df4c1` subtracts 0x28 from the live r0, then pops six words. It
   leaves r0=S+0x70 and SP=S+0x200; its pop can set r2=0 and
   r3=0x0818a911.
4. Two `0x080f8a33` entries subtract 0x18 each and pop one word each. They
   leave r0=P=S+0x40 and SP=S+0x208. Each `pop {pc}` consumes one word,
   four bytes; the earlier ledger mistakenly charged eight bytes per gadget.
5. The call/pop trampoline calls `0x0818a911`, which sets r1=r0=P and returns
   to `0x08030cbe`. That pop consumes four words and leaves SP=S+0x218; set
   r3=0x082721f1 and PC=0x08030cbd.
6. The second trampoline call invokes `FUN_082721f0(P,P,0)`. The function's
   push/pop is balanced, so its return to `0x08030cbe` preserves the external
   SP. The trampoline pop consumes four words and leaves SP=S+0x228; its PC
   can enter `0x0800396d` for the read stage.

The third stack-relative stage therefore starts at S+0x228, not S+0x250,
based on the documented second-stage gadgets and the balanced open ABI above.
The corrected count is 0x28 bytes below the earlier estimate.

7. At S+0x228, `0x0800396d` sets r0=S+0x228; its pop consumes six words and
   leaves SP=S+0x240.
8. Two `0x08005cc3` entries subtract 0xe0 each and pop four words each.
   They leave r0=S+0x68 and SP=S+0x260.
9. `0x081df4c1` subtracts 0x28 and pops six words, setting
   r1=0x10f80, r2=0x09000000, r3=0x08268c3b, and PC=0x08030cbd. It leaves
   r0=P=S+0x40 and SP=S+0x278.
10. The trampoline calls `FUN_08268c3a(P,0x10f80,0x09000000)`. Its push/pop is
    balanced. On return, the trampoline pop consumes words at S+0x278 through
    S+0x287 and leaves SP=S+0x288. Set r3=0x09000000 and PC=0x08030cbd;
    the next `blx r3` enters the loader at the even ARM address 0x09000000.

The final trampoline-pop words are at S+0x278 (r3), S+0x27c (r4), S+0x280
(r5), and S+0x284 (PC). The last required controlled word is therefore at
field data offset 0x280, so the candidate `0x44` field needs at least 0x284
data bytes. Keeping the existing registry tail (4-byte `0x44` end marker,
16-byte `0x2d` record, and two 4-byte container ends) gives a 0x2c0-byte file.
This supersedes both earlier SP/length estimates; it does not prove the file
can be opened, read, or installed. The separately listed `0x08252029`
`sub r0,#8` gadget is not needed after correcting the `pop {pc}` stack cost.

## Limits

The stream ABI and these gadget effects are statically checked against the
runtime image. A complete `0x44` candidate is now serialized locally as
`candidate_accessories_reg_unexecuted.bin` (SHA-256 recorded in `SHA256SUMS`);
the serializer asserts the critical PC words and read arguments above. This
checks the bytes against the static ledger only, not runtime execution. The
actual VFS/provider open/read, persistent placement of a root `/BOOT.BIN`,
heap/stack occupancy, and successful Rockbox handoff are unverified. A short
or failed loader read still leaves the candidate chain proceeding to the
loader address.
