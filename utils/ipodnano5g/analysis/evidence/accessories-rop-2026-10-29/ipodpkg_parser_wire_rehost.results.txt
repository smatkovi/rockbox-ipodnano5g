Limits:

  is a controlled local file-copy model; VFS open, FAT lookup, cluster walk,
  cache and ATA/block I/O are not run in this combined harness.
- The test does not prove that /BOOT.BIN exists or opens on the iPod.
- The load and BSS target are in OSOS's documented managed heap. The actual
  runtime heap frontier and MainAppTask stack address are not captured.
- The test does not run the Rockbox CRT or establish a device boot, persistence,
  lock-switch behavior, or an untethered boot.
- No device, USB, DFU, NAND, NOR, or flash operation occurred. Neither the
  candidate nor the loader package was written to the iPod.
